Biography

I have been a PhD student at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) since September 2024, supervised by Guotai Wang and Shaoting Zhang. I received my bachelor’s degree in electronic engineering from UESTC in 2022. My research focuses on computational pathology and medical AI.
